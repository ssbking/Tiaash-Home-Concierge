Wall Finishes

Wall Finish Guide: Choose What's Right for You

Paint - ₹55/sq ft

✅ Pros

  • Most economical option
  • Wide color range (1000+ shades)
  • Quick application (2-3 days)
  • Easy to touch up later
  • Can be changed frequently
  • Low maintenance - just wipe cleaning

❌ Cons

  • Less durable (5-7 years)
  • Prone to wall marks/scuffs
  • No texture or dimension
  • Fades over time with sunlight
  • Moisture issues in bathrooms
  • Shows wall imperfections
Best for: Bedrooms, living rooms, rental properties, budget projects
Wallpaper - ₹145/sq ft

✅ Pros

  • Vast patterns, textures, designs
  • Hides wall imperfections well
  • Adds warmth and character
  • Durable (8-12 years)
  • Washable options available
  • Quick installation
  • Can create feature walls

❌ Cons

  • Higher cost than paint
  • Difficult removal
  • Seams may show over time
  • Not moisture-proof (except vinyl)
  • Can peel at edges
  • Repairing damage is hard
  • Professional installation needed
Best for: Feature walls, bedrooms, living rooms, dining areas
Types: Vinyl, non-woven, paper, textile, 3D effect
Wall Panels - ₹280/sq ft

✅ Pros

  • Premium luxury look
  • Excellent sound insulation
  • Very durable (15-20 years)
  • Hides all wall imperfections
  • Easy to clean
  • Moisture resistant options
  • Can hide wiring/conduits
  • Adds thermal insulation

❌ Cons

  • Most expensive option
  • Reduces room size slightly
  • Professional installation only
  • Longer installation time
  • Cannot change frequently
  • Repairs require specialists
Best for: TV walls, headboard walls, luxury homes, offices
Materials: MDF, PVC, WPC, 3D panels, acoustic, wood veneer
